O Be Wise
By President D. Todd Christofferson
Second Counselor in the First Presidency

At different times, President Dallin H. Oaks has reminded us that “as we make current decisions, we should always be asking, 'Where will this lead?'' … What is the effect on our future of the decisions we make in the present?” President Oaks points out that each choice has an “opportunity cost,” that is, “if we spend time doing one thing, we lose the opportunity to do another.”

This made me realize how I should also quantify the cost and effect of my every decision and action. I found that not only can we end up gabling away our money, we could also gamble away our time. As someone who gambles with time instead of money, I begin to realize the cost of every time spent on mindless gacha games or time spent looking at my screen. I end with this qoute; 'Do not procrastinate the day of your repentance.'
