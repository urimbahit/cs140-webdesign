What is VALORANT?

VALORANT is a 5 vs 5 tactical first-person shooter (FPS) game made by Riot Games. It combines CSGO-like gunplay and teamwork but with the added twist of unique character abilities. There are different Agents or characters, each with their own abilities and roles. The goal of the game would be to either plant and defend from the Spike (a bomb) or eliminate the opposing team. Aside from its competitive gameplay, VALORANT also features an extensive fictional universe (LORE) that explores the origins, abilities, and conflicts of its Agents.

Agent Roles

Agents are divided into four roles: Duelists, Controllers, Initiators, and Sentinels.

Role Description
Duelist Specialize in taking fights, entering objectivess, and creating opportunities for team entry.
Controller Manipulate sightlines and control territory using smokes, walls, and other vision-blocking abilities.
Initiator Gather information and disrupt enemy positions to help teammates enter contested areas.
Sentinel Focus on defense, surveillance, and protecting territory from enemy advances.

Functional Ability Types

Abilities can be classified into different functional types: Vision Blockers, Flashes / Blinds, Damage / Mollies, Intel / Recon, Crowd Control & Debuffs, and Buffs & Sustain.

Ability Type Description
Vision Blockers Smokes and walls that obstruct enemy sightlines.
Flashes / Blinds Abilities that temporarily impair enemy vision.
Damage / Mollies Abilities that deal damage over an area or force enemies out of positions.
Movement Dashes, teleports, and other abilities used for repositioning.
Intel / Recon Abilities that reveal or track enemy positions.
Crowd Control & Debuffs Abilities that stun, slow, tether, or restrict enemy actions.
Buffs & Sustain Abilities that provide healing, shielding, revives, or other advantages.

General Lore: Radiants and the VALORANT Universe

VALORANT takes place in a futuristic Earth following an event known as First Light, which led to the discovery of Radianite and the emergence of individuals called Radiants. Radiants possess extraordinary supernatural abilities, while other Agents rely on advanced technology and combat expertise. The VALORANT Protocol is an organization composed of these individuals, tasked with protecting the world from threats involving Radianite. Its central conflict involves two parallel Earths, Alpha and Omega, whose opposing interests in Radianite drive much of the game's storyline.
