History of The PQCNM

In 1961 Missionary work officially began in the Philippines when the first four missionaries arrived in Pasay City. The Philippines Quezon City North Mission was officially established on July 1, 2011. The creation of the mission was announced by the Church in February 2011 to accommodate rapid member growth in the region. It was formed directly from a division of the Philippines Manila and Philippines Quezon City missions.

My Mission Parents

President Mark A. Workman and Sister Janis K. Workman served as the mission leaders of the Philippines Quezon City North Mission from July 2023 through June 2026. Learn More

Originally from Yuma, Arizona, they were called by the Church to succeed President Todd C. Liston and Sister Kara Liston. Before their assignment in Quezon City, President Workman had a deep history with missionary work in the country, having previously served as a young missionary himself in the Philippines Baguio Mission. I would also like to note that I lived in Baguio, and I was in the first batch of missionaries they welcomed in the mission. In July 2026, they completed their three-year term of service and were succeeded by President Jeffrey H. Winter and Sister Paige A. Winter

Since I was part of the first batch of missionaies the Workmans welcomed into the mission, when it came time to leave the mission, there were special tears shed, we were the first they had handled, and that also signified their last remaining year of service.
