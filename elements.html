Songs of the Philippines

The Philippines is famous for many original songwritters and music genre.
OPM (Original Pilipino Music) spans legendary classics and trending modern hits that dominate local charts.

Iconic Filipino Classics

  1. "Dahil Sa Iyo" - by Mike Velarde & Pilita Corrales
  2. "Ang Huling El Bimbo" - by Eraserheads
  3. "Manila" - by Hotdog
  4. "Bayan Ko" - by Freddie Aguilar & Various Artists
  5. "Maalaala Mo Kaya" - by Dulce & Various Artists
