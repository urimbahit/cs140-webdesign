FUN FACT:

Before it was called Baguio, the area was known as Kafagway, an Ibaloi term meaning "wide open space" or grassy clearing. The word bagiw (moss) was adapted and Hispanicized into Baguio during the American period. Natives and residents of the city are called Ibagiw, which also stems from the root word bagiw
